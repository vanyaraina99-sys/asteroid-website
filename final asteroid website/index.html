Asteroids

What is the first thing you think of when you hear the word "asteroid"? It's probably something related to space or rock, maybe even space rock! But, it probably wasn't economical target or something you could profit from in general. An asteroid's orbital parameters, which determine how easy it is to reach and mine an asteroid, and its composition, or what it's actually made of, helps determine the value of an asteroid to see if it's worth mining. There are many different parameters like an asteroids semi-major axis, eccentricity, inclination, etc, and there are also three asteroid type which are determined by what they are made of. These three types are C-type, S-type, and M-type asteroids. There are so many oppurtunities to be explore with asteroids, but you need to know what asteroids TRULY are first. Asteroids are leftover space rocks that never gathered into a planet when the solar system formed about 4.6 billion years ago. Most of them are in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. They also greaty vary in sizes and can be anywhere from the size of a pebble, 940km across(like Ceres), or way bigger! Even though most of the asteroids that we know of are in this Mars-Jupiter belt, some of them even go past Neptune!
